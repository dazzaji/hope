# Reconciliation Ledger — SB 574 Multi-Agent Research

## How to read this ledger

This is the correction-and-integration record from the `peer-synthi` phase of the SB 574 project (see [`README.md`](README.md)). When two agents research the same question independently and then combine their work, the honest way to merge is not to average their prose — it is to take each material finding and record, one by one, what happened to it. That is what this ledger does.

**The candor here is the point, not an embarrassment.** A healthy multi-agent record shows findings that were integrated, findings that had to be *qualified*, overclaims that were *corrected* or *rejected*, source checks that *failed*, and issues left *deferred*. Deleting those would make the work look cleaner while destroying the evidence that review actually happened.

**Stable finding IDs.** Each finding carries a short, stable ID so it can be tracked from its first appearance through to its final disposition, even as the narrative combines or splits claims. The ID families here refer to:

- **C##** — frozen findings contributed by the Codex agent;
- **Claude findings-ledger IDs** — the frozen findings contributed by the Claude agent;
- **GAP-##** — gaps the agents identified in the combined record;
- **NS##** — new synthesis produced during reconciliation;
- **table contributions** (A/B/C/E series) — findings destined for specific tables; and
- **DEL-##** — changes recorded during delivered-version review.

(These prefixes are used as the source materials used them; no undocumented meaning is added here.)

**The four columns.**

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Original item** | The finding's stable ID (and any split, where a compound claim was separated). |
| **Destination** | Where it landed in the final report — section(s) and/or table(s). |
| **Disposition** | What happened to it: `integrate`, `qualify`, `correct`, `defer`, `reject`, and similar. |
| **Reason / preserved value** | Why — the substantive judgment, including what was kept, limited, or thrown out. |

A disposition such as **integrate** means the finding was carried into the report; **qualify** means it was kept but bounded; **correct** means an error or overclaim was fixed; **defer** means it was left open for later; a rejection means the agents declined to assert it. Differences in confidence are preserved deliberately.

**What a workshop participant should do:** you are not expected to read all of this. Spot-check a handful of representative rows — pick a few `integrate` rows, then find a `correct`, `qualify`, or rejected row — to see that the agents preserved provenance and disagreement rather than silently smoothing everything into agreement.

*This ledger is reproduced verbatim from the project's working reconciliation record; only obvious spacing/rendering defects were fixed, and no private paths or links were added. It is a historical artifact of the September 2026 research run.*
